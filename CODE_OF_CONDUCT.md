# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be civil. Discuss the program, not the person.

## What is expected

- Keep issues and comments on topic and factual.
- Accept that the author decides what goes into the project.
- Remember that the people reading are volunteers, not support staff.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Deliberate derailing of discussions.

## Enforcement

The author may edit or delete comments, close issues, and block accounts
that break these rules. Serious or repeated breaches lead to a permanent
block without further discussion.
